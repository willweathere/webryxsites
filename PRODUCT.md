# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

UK small-business owners (restaurants, trades, gyms, salons, local services) who need a website that wins customers but have no time or technical background. They arrive from word of mouth or local search, usually on a phone, and decide within a minute whether this agency feels professional and affordable. The job to be done: "get me a credible site (and socials) without jargon, risk, or a big agency price tag."

## Product Purpose

Rune Sites (runewebsites.com, formerly Webryx Sites) is a lead-generation marketing site for a web design agency. Every section exists to move a visitor toward one of three conversions: pick a fixed package (£99/£249/£499 + monthly), buy a social-media plan (£50–£150/mo), or complete the 6-step "build your own site" enquiry form. Leads are stored in Supabase and emailed to the owner. Success = enquiries submitted.

## Brand Personality

Confident, plain-spoken, premium-but-approachable. The site must practice what it preaches: it sells "modern, fast, conversion-focused websites," so it has to *be* one. Dark, glowing, indigo/violet aesthetic (committed brand identity — preserve it). Voice avoids jargon, leads with prices and outcomes, and reassures constantly (no contracts, reply in 24h, you own your site).

## Anti-references

- Generic AI-generated SaaS landing pages: uppercase tracked eyebrow labels above every section, gradient text headlines, identical icon-card grids.
- Corporate agency sites that hide pricing behind "book a call."
- Cluttered DIY-builder templates — the "before" site in the transformation slider is the literal anti-reference.

## Design Principles

1. **Practice what you preach** — the site is the portfolio; every detail (speed, mobile feel, polish) is proof of competence.
2. **Price up front, friction down** — pricing, packages, and the form are the heroes; never bury a CTA.
3. **Show, don't tell** — code-rendered mockups, the before/after slider, and the phone visual demonstrate capability instead of claiming it.
4. **Phone-first** — most prospects arrive on mobile; every interaction must be thumb-friendly and fast on cheap Android GPUs.
5. **Reassure relentlessly** — 24h reply, no contracts, you own it; trust cues sit beside every ask.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA: body text ≥4.5:1 on the dark theme, visible focus states, full keyboard support for interactive widgets (FAQ, slider, form), `prefers-reduced-motion` alternatives for all animation, content never gated behind JS-triggered reveals.
